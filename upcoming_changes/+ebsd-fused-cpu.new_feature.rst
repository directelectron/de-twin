EBSD camera frames are made in one fused CPU pass, a few frames per job on a pool of below-normal-priority worker threads (``DE_TWIN_FRAME_THREADS``): ~7,800 frames/s from ``twin.frames`` and ~5,500-7,200 through the shared-memory face for a 512x512 binned DESim frame on a 48-thread machine (was ~750). The frames match the general path's except where float rounding tips a draw; ``DE_TWIN_FUSED_EBSD=0`` selects the general path.
